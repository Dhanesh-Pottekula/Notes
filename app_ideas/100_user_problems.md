# 100 everyday problems people would pay an app to fix (India-first)

Each row: **what people feel** → what they wish existed → the app that could come out of it → who already
does something like it. These come from common complaints, not from a survey; treat them as leads to check
against real Play Store 1-star reviews and Reddit threads (r/india, r/IndianStreetBets, r/developersIndia,
r/bangalore) before building.

"Exists" means some app already covers part of it. A crowded row can still work if the existing apps are
cluttered, ad-heavy, or bad at the Indian case — that's usually where the 1-star reviews point.

## How to pick one

Score each idea 1–5 on:
1. **Pain:** do people complain about it often and angrily?
2. **Who pays:** will someone pay, or is it ad-only?
3. **Buildable alone:** can one developer ship it in 4–6 weeks?
4. **Data access:** does it need data the platform blocks (WhatsApp, iOS Screen Time, bank APIs)?
5. **Competition:** are existing apps weak at the Indian case?

Then check the top 5 against real 1-star reviews of the apps in the "Exists?" column.
