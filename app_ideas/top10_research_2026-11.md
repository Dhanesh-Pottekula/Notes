# Top 10 app ideas checked against real evidence (researched 2 Nov 2026)

Follow-up to `100_user_problems.md`. Five research agents checked the 10 shortlisted ideas on the web:
official numbers, competitor apps on the Play Store (India listings, read 2 Nov 2026), their recurring
complaints, and any sign people pay.

**Big limit:** Reddit was blocked for every agent (search and fetch), so real user voices are thin;
they come from Blind, news stories, Play Store reviews and studies instead. "unverified" = seen only in a
search summary or a third-party page.

## The pattern

- **Where the pain is biggest, a giant gives the fix away free:** scams (Truecaller, Google), train
  waitlists (IRCTC, ixigo/ConfirmTkt), UPI mandates (NPCI).
- **Where people already pay, the field is crowded:** calorie apps (HealthifyMe, Cal AI), screen-time apps
  (Opal, one sec, ScreenZen, BrainPal).
- **Ideas no one is doing are often unpaid for a reason:** document vault, maid attendance, work-wins.
- **Implication:** win on a narrow gap the leader leaves, or bundle so someone with money pays.

## Evidence per idea

### #13 Scam Check for parents
- Losses Rs 22,845.73 crore in 2024 (up from Rs 7,465 crore in 2023), 36.37 lakh incidents on NCRP —
  government figures via Scroll, 2025. Rs 19,813 crore in 2025 — I4C via IANS, Jan 2026.
- Digital-arrest incidents 39,925 (2022) → 123,672 (2024) — IndiaSpend on NCRP data.
- User voice: a Blind poster whose parents sent about Rs 50 lakh to a fake "your son is in custody" caller (Dec 2023).
- **Competition:** Truecaller Scam Checker (launched end of Sep 2026) — free, checks pasted text, links and
  numbers; **no screenshots yet ("Text only for now")**. Google on-device scam detection (Pixel 9+ only in
  India). Sanchar Saathi/Chakshu lets you report, not check. ScamMukt ~Rs 50/month (traction unverified).
- **Pay:** Truecaller Premium Rs 79/month; ~4M payers out of ~350M Indian users.
- **Blockers:** Play Store lets only default SMS/phone apps read SMS, so no auto-scan; WhatsApp gives no
  access — parent must forward or screenshot; liability if a scam is marked "SAFE"; DPDP Act.
- **Gap left:** screenshots, Indian languages, an alert to the child, an elder-first UI.

### #16 Daily "I'm okay" check-in
- 5.7% of people 60+ live alone (9.0% of women) — LASI Wave 1 (2017–18). Loneliness about 37% among them.
- "Are You Dead?" (Demumu) became the #1 paid iPhone app in China in Jan 2026 (~8 yuan).
- **Competition:** Snug Safety (iOS ~4.9; complaints of false alarms), HaalChaal, KinStatus, CheckinBee
  ($14/month); Emoha and Samarth sell 24/7 emergency response.
- **Pay:** Samarth ~Rs 9,999/month care plans; Privilege plan Rs 4,800/year — families pay for human help.
- **Blockers:** phone makers' battery savers kill reminders (false alarms); elders drop the daily habit;
  liability if an alert fails.

### #24 Scroll Wrapped / reels counter
- Indian short-video use ~38–45 min/day (Redseer 2022, Bain 2022).
- **Competition (Play India):** BrainPal (ex "BrainRot: Reels Counter") 4.5★, 79K reviews, 10L+ installs —
  counts reels, limits, friend battles; ScreenZen 4.6★ 10L+; one sec 4.5★ 10L+.
- **Complaints:** locks too easy to bypass, paywalled features, stops working after updates, privacy unease.
- **Pay:** Opal revenue estimated ~$4.8M (2024, third-party estimate).
- **Blockers:** Android Accessibility policy (prominent disclosure + Play declaration); iOS cannot count reels;
  counters break when Instagram/YouTube change their UI.
- **Gap left:** a strict lock you can't cheat, a better shareable "Wrapped".

### #43 Indian-food calorie app
- HealthifyMe's own photo accuracy ~60–70% (TechCrunch 2023) to ~80% (OpenAI case study, unverified);
  struggles with multi-item plates — the thali case.
- **Competition (Play India):** HealthifyMe 4.4★ 1Cr+ (complaints: wrong counts, slow fixes, refunds);
  Cal AI 4.4★ 10L+ (inaccurate logging, paywall after signup); MyFitnessPal 4.5★ 10Cr+.
- **Pay:** Cal AI ~$35M run rate (May 2025, founder-reported); reportedly acquired by MyFitnessPal in 2026 (unverified).
- **Blockers:** photos can't see oil/ghee/portions; India pays less per user.
- **Gap left:** thali and portion accuracy.

### #2 Subscription / UPI AutoPay tracker
- Complaints of Rs 1–2 trials becoming Rs 149–699 auto-debits (Kuku FM, Stage) — Outlook Money, May 2025;
  Seekho charging after cancel — The Ken, May 2025; mandates on a mother's phone — Asianet, Jul 2026.
- RBI E-mandate Framework 2026 (21 Apr 2026): 24-hour pre-debit notice; opt out of one debit or the mandate.
- **Competition:** NPCI UPI Help can view/pause/revoke all mandates from any UPI app (rolling out);
  GPay/PhonePe/Paytm show their own mandates; Rocket Money doesn't work in India.
- **Blockers:** SMS reading allowed for "SMS-based money management" with Google review; iOS no SMS;
  a third-party app can't revoke a mandate (needs UPI PIN).

### #91 Train waitlist predictor
- Waitlisted passengers who couldn't travel: 2.72 crore (FY23) → 3.39 crore (FY26) (RTI via NDTV/Curly Tales).
- **Competition:** ConfirmTkt 4.9★ 10Cr+, ixigo Trains 4.8★ 10Cr+, RailYatri, IRCTC (CRIS prediction since 2018).
- **Pay:** people pay for refund guarantees (ixigo 2X–3X), not predictions.
- **Blockers:** only licensed agents sell tickets; IRCTC terms bar automated access; no historical data.

### #44 Lab report explainer
- ~35–38% of 110 inpatients couldn't understand their results (SRM study).
- **Competition:** small paid apps (ReportSaathi 10K+, others ≤1L installs, Rs 29–4,300); labs building it
  in-house (Healthians, PharmEasy); free ChatGPT/Gemini. ChatGPT Health is US-only.
- **Blockers:** Telemedicine Guidelines 2020 (AI can't counsel/prescribe); CDSCO software-as-medical-device
  rules (unverified); Play health declaration; DPDP.

### #63 Document vault
- ~56% of registered vehicles uninsured (Parliamentary committee 2024–25 via ETV Bharat); PUC compliance
  <30% in several states (CARS24, vendor data); Rs 10,000 PUC fine in Delhi.
- **Competition:** DigiLocker (~3.9★, govt docs only, no reminders); Park+ (10M+) already sends vehicle
  insurance/PUC/challan alerts; warranty trackers with almost no reviews.
- **Pay:** none found.

### #53 Maid attendance & salary
- One reported Bengaluru dispute (News Karnataka, Feb 2025); developer copy describes month-end arguments.
- **Competition:** MyGate 4.7★ 50L+ (attendance + pay, no leave/advance; ad complaints); several free niche
  apps at 50–1K installs.
- **Pay:** none found.

### #37 Work-wins journal
- Julia Evans's brag-document post (2019) names the pain.
- **Pay:** BragJournal (this exact product) shows $12 MRR, 2 subscribers, $174 all-time (TrustMRR).
- Free templates and ChatGPT do the job; enterprise tools (Lattice, Confirm) own the paid side.

## What to do with this

1. **Bundle the parents' ideas (#13 + #16 + medicine reminders) into one "parents' safety" app the child
   pays for.** Separately each scores 9–10 with weak payment; together it matches what families already buy
   (elder-care plans), and it targets the gap Truecaller leaves: screenshots, Indian languages, child alerts.
2. **Or take a proven-money category with a narrow gap:** a reels blocker you can't cheat (#24) or thali
   accuracy (#43). Expect strong competition.
3. **Before building, get the missing user voices:** read Reddit (r/india, r/bangalore, r/IndianFitness)
   by hand and talk to 10 people in the target group.
