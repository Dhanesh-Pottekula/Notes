# 3. Behavioral, leadership and HR rounds

Startups and product companies give these rounds real weight: they check ownership, judgement and whether people
want to work with you. Prepare 8–10 STAR stories (2 minutes each, a number in the result) and map every question
below to one of them; the hints name the story from your own work that fits. Story bank: `../interview-topics/12-behavioral.md`.

## 3.1 Tell me about yourself — your career story
**Learn:** `../application-answers.md` "Tell us about yourself (60 seconds)" · `../interview-topics/12-behavioral.md` "12.2 Common questions" · https://www.techinterviewhandbook.org/self-introduction/
- [ ] `L1` Tell me about yourself. ↳ now → before → why this role; 60–90 seconds, one number
- [ ] `L1` Walk me through your resume, job by job.
- [ ] `L1` You studied civil engineering. How did you end up in software? ↳ self-taught from 2023, projects, StreetDine in final year
- [ ] `L1` What does your current role involve, day to day?
- [ ] `L1` Describe yourself in three words, with an example for each.
- [ ] `L2` Why did you join StreetDine while still in college? ↳ founding team, real users, learn by shipping
- [ ] `L2` Why did you move from StreetDine to Jigsaw?
- [ ] `L2` Why did you move from Jigsaw to AiRA? ↳ founding role, AI-first product, more ownership
- [ ] `L2` What did each job teach you that the previous one didn't?
- [ ] `L2` How did you learn backend engineering without a CS degree? Which gaps did you have to close? ↳ name each gap and how you closed it
- [ ] `L2` Does your civil engineering background help you as an engineer? How?
- [ ] `L2` Which one project best shows how you work? ↳ Lumen or the AiRA voice rewrite
- [ ] `L2` Tell me your story as if I were a non-technical founder.
- [ ] `L3` Your degree isn't in CS. Why should we trust your fundamentals? ↳ shipped systems, open source, concepts you can be drilled on
- [ ] `L3` Three companies in under three years. Why should we believe you'll stay? ↳ a clear reason per move; what you want long term
- [ ] `L3` What would your Jigsaw manager say about you? What would your CEO at AiRA say?

## 3.3 Leadership principles I — ownership, bias for action, dive deep, deliver results
**Learn:** https://www.amazon.jobs/content/en/our-workplace/leadership-principles · `../interview-topics/12-behavioral.md` "12.1 Your story bank" · https://www.techinterviewhandbook.org/behavioral-interview/
- [ ] `L1` What is the STAR format, and why do interviewers want it?
- [ ] `L1` Tell me about something you owned end to end. ↳ AiRA voice rewrite or Lumen: idea → production → after launch
- [ ] `L2` Tell me about a time you took on something outside your job description.
- [ ] `L2` Tell me about a time you fixed a problem nobody asked you to fix. ↳ StreetDine: 4 report pipelines → 1, −715 lines
- [ ] `L2` Tell me about a time you decided quickly with incomplete information. ↳ cost of waiting vs being wrong; was it reversible?
- [ ] `L2` Tell me about a time you built a small prototype to learn before building the real thing. ↳ JSON UI engine prototype and its written comparison
- [ ] `L2` Tell me about a time you dug into the details to find the real cause of a problem. ↳ Jigsaw report 12 min → 30 s
- [ ] `L2` Tell me about a time the data contradicted what everyone believed. ↳ trading backtests: popular strategies lost after costs
- [ ] `L2` Tell me about a time you delivered under a tight deadline. What did you cut? ↳ StatusPulse in 3 days
- [ ] `L2` Tell me about a time you missed a deadline. What happened, and what did you change?
- [ ] `L2` What is your biggest measurable impact? ↳ voice: 3 pipelines and 17,000 lines replaced; or Lumen's cost per video
- [ ] `L2` Tell me about a time you kept going after a serious setback.
- [ ] `L2` Tell me about a time a guarantee mattered to users, like "this fires at most once". ↳ crash-safe reminders with Redis locks
- [ ] `L3` Tell me about a time ownership meant cleaning up something you didn't break.
- [ ] `L3` Tell me about a time acting fast was the wrong call.
- [ ] `L3` How do you know when to stop digging and ship?

## 3.4 Leadership principles II — customers, trust, simplicity, disagreement, developing people
**Learn:** https://www.amazon.jobs/content/en/our-workplace/leadership-principles · https://staffeng.com/guides/ · `../interview-topics/12-behavioral.md` "12.5 Ownership and product questions"
- [ ] `L1` Who are your users today, and how do you hear from them?
- [ ] `L2` Tell me about a time you changed a design because of user feedback.
- [ ] `L2` Tell me about a time you protected users even though it slowed you down. ↳ agent permissions + credential vault; "never trust the browser with money"
- [ ] `L2` Tell me about a time you simplified a complex system. ↳ 3 voice pipelines → one WebSocket; 4 report pipelines → 1
- [ ] `L2` Tell me about something new you invented. ↳ Lumen: the model writes a script, not pixels
- [ ] `L2` Tell me about a time you disagreed with your manager or CEO and then committed anyway.
- [ ] `L2` Tell me about a time you pushed back and turned out to be right. Now one where you were wrong.
- [ ] `L2` Tell me about a time you admitted you were wrong in front of the team.
- [ ] `L2` How do you earn trust with a new team or a new manager?
- [ ] `L2` Tell me about a time you raised the quality bar for the team. ↳ TypeScript migration, PR review rules, CI checks
- [ ] `L2` Tell me about someone you helped grow. What changed for them? ↳ 3 mentees at Jigsaw; your AiRA team
- [ ] `L2` Tell me about a time you stopped using a number because you couldn't back it up. ↳ dropped an unmeasured resume metric; correct it fast
- [ ] `L3` Tell me about a time a simple solution beat a clever one.
- [ ] `L3` Tell me about a time you said no to a stakeholder's request. How did you explain it?
- [ ] `L3` What does "high standards" mean at a startup that has to ship fast?
- [ ] `L3` Tell me about a time you gave credit to someone else for work that made you look good.

## 3.5 Conflict, failure and mistakes
**Learn:** https://sre.google/sre-book/postmortem-culture/ · https://www.techinterviewhandbook.org/behavioral-interview-questions/ · *Crucial Conversations* (Patterson et al.)
- [ ] `L1` Tell me about a conflict with a teammate. How was it resolved?
- [ ] `L1` Tell me about a failure. What did you learn?
- [ ] `L1` What is the biggest mistake you've made at work?
- [ ] `L2` Tell me about a time a teammate reverted or rewrote your work. How did you handle it? ↳ Fieldom Stream: fix merged, reverted, then solved differently
- [ ] `L2` Tell me about a disagreement on a technical approach. How was it decided? ↳ data, a prototype, a timebox, who owns the call
- [ ] `L2` Tell me about working with someone difficult.
- [ ] `L2` Tell me about a time you got harsh feedback. What did you do with it?
- [ ] `L2` Tell me about a bug you shipped to production. ↳ what broke, how it was found, the fix, what changed after
- [ ] `L2` Tell me about a time you skipped or deleted tests to move faster. Was it worth it?
- [ ] `L2` Tell me about a time a refactor silently broke something. How did you find out? ↳ Perplexity Mini retrieval regression; add evals
- [ ] `L2` Tell me about a project that failed or was cancelled.
- [ ] `L3` Tell me about a conflict with someone more senior than you.
- [ ] `L3` Two people on your team disagree strongly. How do you, as lead, resolve it?
- [ ] `L3` What mistake do you keep making, and what are you doing about it?
- [ ] `L3` Tell me about a technology choice you got wrong.

## 3.6 Production incidents, deadlines, prioritisation and ambiguity
**Learn:** https://sre.google/sre-book/managing-incidents/ · https://staffeng.com/guides/work-on-what-matters · AIE ch1 "Milestone Planning" (pdf p57)
- [ ] `L1` Tell me about a production incident you handled.
- [ ] `L1` How do you prioritise when everything is urgent?
- [ ] `L2` Walk me through your first 15 minutes when production breaks. ↳ impact, tell people, mitigate or roll back, then root cause
- [ ] `L2` How do you write a postmortem? What makes it blameless?
- [ ] `L2` Tell me about a time a fix for one bug caused another.
- [ ] `L2` Tell me about a time you were paged late at night. What did you change afterwards?
- [ ] `L2` How do you choose between a quick patch and a proper fix?
- [ ] `L2` Two deadlines clash. How do you choose?
- [ ] `L2` A deadline is impossible. What do you say, and to whom? ↳ early, with options: cut scope, move date, accept risk
- [ ] `L2` Tell me about a time the requirements changed halfway through.
- [ ] `L2` You get a vague request. How do you turn it into a plan? ↳ user, success metric, smallest version, a checkpoint
- [ ] `L2` How do you decide what not to build?
- [ ] `L2` How do you start work nobody on the team has done before? ↳ Lumen or realtime voice: read, spike, prototype, measure
- [ ] `L3` Something breaks in the middle of an investor demo. Walk me through it.
- [ ] `L3` How do you get tech-debt work onto the roadmap?
- [ ] `L3` How do you estimate work that is mostly unknown? ↳ spike first, ranges not points, re-estimate at checkpoints

## 3.7 Team lead and founding engineer

- [ ] `L1` What does a normal week look like for you as team lead?
- [ ] `L2` How do you run PR reviews? What do you block on, and what do you let go? ↳ block on correctness, security, data; style via linters
- [ ] `L2` How do you own system design and still write code yourself?
- [ ] `L2` How do you work with the CEO? How do you turn a founder's idea into a plan? ↳ find the user problem, propose the smallest version, show trade-offs
- [ ] `L2` Tell me about a time you pushed back on the CEO. ↳ data and options first; then commit
- [ ] `L2` How do you give hard feedback to someone on your team? Give a real example. ↳ private, specific, behaviour + impact, agreed next step
- [ ] `L2` Someone on your team is underperforming. What do you do?

- [ ] `L2` Have you interviewed or hired anyone? How do you run an interview? ↳ a task close to the real job; rubric; look at reasoning
- [ ] `L2` How do you split work across a 4-person frontend and backend team?
- [ ] `L2` How do you keep code quality up when everyone is moving fast? ↳ review rules, CI, tests on risky paths, clear owners
- [ ] `L3` Two engineers want to build the same thing in two different ways. How do you decide?

## 3.8 Working with AI tools
**Learn:** https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/claude-code-best-practices · https://docs.anthropic.com/en/docs/claude-code/sub-agents · https://simonwillison.net/tags/ai-assisted-programming/
- [ ] `L1` Which AI coding tools do you use, and for what?
- [ ] `L1` What do you never let an AI tool do without checking?
- [ ] `L2` Walk me through building a feature with Claude Code. ↳ spec → plan → task-sized commits → review each diff → tests
- [ ] `L2` How do you review AI-written code? What do you look for first? ↳ wrong assumptions, deleted tests, stale docs, security, edge cases
- [ ] `L2` Tell me about a time an AI tool produced something wrong that looked right. ↳ docs describing a deleted Redux layer; tests removed in a commit
- [ ] `L2` Some of your side projects were mostly agent-built. How do you show you own that code? ↳ you wrote the specs, reviewed diffs, can defend each design
- [ ] `L2` How do you keep secrets and customer data out of AI tools?
- [ ] `L2` How do you use subagents or several agents at once? ↳ learning-os: one agent per job, a checker agent verifies
- [ ] `L2` Has AI made you faster? How do you know? ↳ honest: faster to draft, slower to review
- [ ] `L2` How do you set rules for AI tools across a team? ↳ AGENTS.md/CLAUDE.md conventions, review standards, no blind merges
- [ ] `L2` How do you stop juniors from shipping AI code they don't understand?
- [ ] `L3` An agent writes 17,000 lines in one commit. How do you review it?
- [ ] `L3` Would you let an AI agent merge to main? Under what conditions?
- [ ] `L3` How do you write a spec so an agent builds the right thing? ↳ goal, constraints, examples, acceptance tests, small tasks
- [ ] `L3` Where will AI coding tools be in two years, and how does that change what you learn now?

## 3.12 Salary, notice period and HR trick questions
**Learn:** `../application-brief.md` "Numbers to enter" · https://www.techinterviewhandbook.org/negotiation/ · https://www.techinterviewhandbook.org/understanding-compensation/
- [ ] `L1` What is your current CTC? ↳ state it plainly; split fixed and variable
- [ ] `L1` What is your expected CTC? ↳ give a range; ask their band first
- [ ] `L1` What is your notice period? Can you join earlier? ↳ state it; mention early release if possible
- [ ] `L1` Are you open to relocating? Which cities?
- [ ] `L1` Are you open to working from the office five days a week?
- [ ] `L2` Why is your expectation so much higher than your current salary? ↳ market rate for the role, scope, other offers; stay calm
- [ ] `L2` Our budget is below your range. What now? ↳ ask total comp: ESOPs, bonus, review date; know your floor
- [ ] `L2` How do you compare a startup offer with ESOPs against a higher fixed salary? ↳ vesting, cliff, strike price, liquidity, dilution
- [ ] `L2` Do you have other offers? ↳ honest; share timelines, not necessarily numbers
- [ ] `L2` If your company makes a counter-offer, will you take it? ↳ you decided on reasons, not money; say so
- [ ] `L2` Your GitHub shows StreetDine commits during your Jigsaw dates. Explain. ↳ part-time on StreetDine after Aug 2024; one honest line
- [ ] `L2` You joined StreetDine before finishing your degree. How did you manage both?
- [ ] `L2` Your resume shows nothing between April and August 2024. What were you doing? ↳ graduation, job search, side projects from that window
- [ ] `L2` Why three jobs in under three years? ↳ one line per move; moving towards something, not away
- [ ] `L2` Tell me something about you that isn't on your resume.
- [ ] `L2` Can you share payslips and relieving letters for background verification? ↳ keep offer letters, relieving letters, payslips, degree ready
- [ ] `L2` Why should we not hire you?
- [ ] `L2` You lead a team now. Will you be fine in an individual-contributor role? ↳ yes, with reasons; leading without the title
- [ ] `L3` You've accepted another offer and we come back with a better one. What do you do?
- [ ] `L3` Would you sign a service bond or accept a long notice period?
- [ ] `L3` Your manager asks you to do something you think is unethical. What do you do?
- [ ] `L3` Rate yourself out of 10 in Python and in system design. ↳ honest, with evidence; never a 10
