Kafka — topics, partitions, consumer groups, exactly-once

183 questions · L1 53 · L2 87 · L3 43 · 14 sub-topics

Kafka comes up in almost every backend system-design round ("put a queue here — which one and why?"). A 3-year engineer must explain partitions, consumer groups, offsets and delivery guarantees without notes, and know when not to use Kafka. Syllabus: 2D Kafka (../../syllabus.html).

12.2 Topics, partitions and keys

  • L1 Why does a topic have partitions?

  • L1 How does a producer decide which partition a record goes to?

  • L2 What key would you choose for an orders topic? For a chat-messages topic? For a clickstream topic?

  • L2 How many partitions should a topic have? How do you decide?

  • L2 What does a partition look like on disk?

  • L3 What are the costs of too many partitions?

  • L3 Explain the sticky partitioner and why it replaced round-robin for keyless records.
