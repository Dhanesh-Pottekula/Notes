Docker and containers

271 questions · L1 82 · L2 136 · L3 53 · 14 sub-topics

Docker comes up in every backend round that touches deployment: "container vs VM", "how do you make this image smaller", "CMD vs ENTRYPOINT", "why doesn't my container stop cleanly". A 3-year engineer who ran Docker in production (AiRA on Azure/AWS VMs, the Perplexity Mini Compose stack) must explain what a container really is in Linux terms and debug a broken one without guessing. Syllabus: 3A Docker (../../syllabus.html).
