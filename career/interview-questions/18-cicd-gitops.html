294 questions · L1 84 · L2 166 · L3 44 · 15 sub-topics

Almost every round has "walk me through how your code gets to production" and at least one Git question. A 3-year engineer must design a pipeline end to end, explain canary vs blue-green and how to roll back past a DB migration, keep secrets out of CI, and fix Git mistakes without panic.
