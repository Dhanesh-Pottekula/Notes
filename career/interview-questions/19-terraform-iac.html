Terraform and infrastructure as code

195 questions · L1 54 · L2 105 · L3 36 · 11 sub-topics

Interviewers use Terraform to check that you treat infrastructure like code: reviewed, versioned, repeatable. A 3-year engineer must explain state, plan/apply, modules and multi-environment layouts, avoid the classic traps (secrets in state, count reindexing, manual drift), and sketch the Terraform for a real stack. Syllabus: 4B Terraform and IaC (../../syllabus.html).
