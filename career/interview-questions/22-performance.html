Performance engineering — measure, profile, fix, prove it

239 questions · L1 57 · L2 127 · L3 55 · 14 sub-topics

Performance questions check whether you measure before you optimise, can read a flame graph, know where time goes in a request (CPU, I/O, queueing, network, database), and can prove a speed-up with numbers. Expect a deep follow-up chain on any number on your resume — "12 min → 30 s, how?". Syllabus: 4E Performance Engineering (../../syllabus.html).

22.1 Methodology — measure first, latency vs throughput, the laws

  • L1 Latency vs throughput vs bandwidth: define each.

  • L1 What is a bottleneck?
  • L1 What does "premature optimisation is the root of all evil" actually mean, in full context?
  • L2 Can you improve throughput while making latency worse? Give an example.
    batching, bigger queues, Nagle
  • L2 What is Little's law? Use it: 200 req/s at 50 ms average — how many requests are in flight?
    L = λ·W = 10
  • L2 What is Amdahl's law? If 20 % of a job is serial, what is the maximum speed-up?
    5×
  • L2 What happens to latency as utilisation approaches 100 %? Why do people target 60–70 %?

  • L2 What is the USE method, and how do you apply it to find a bottleneck?
  • L2 Walk me through how you would approach "the API is slow" with no other information.
  • L2 What latency numbers should every engineer know?
    RAM ~100 ns, SSD ~100 µs, same-DC RTT ~0.5 ms, cross-continent ~150 ms
  • L2 What is a performance budget, and how do you split one across the stages of a request?
  • L2 CPU-bound vs I/O-bound vs memory-bound: how do you tell which one you are?
  • L3 What is the Universal Scalability Law, and why does throughput sometimes go down as you add nodes?
    contention + coherency cost
  • L3 How do you prove an optimisation worked and wasn't noise?
    repeated runs, same env, variance, percentiles, before/after in prod metrics
  • L3 What is the anti-method of "streetlight" debugging, and how do you avoid it?
