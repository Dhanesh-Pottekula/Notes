LLM fundamentals and AI engineering

417 questions · L1 103 · L2 212 · L3 102 · 22 sub-topics

AI-engineer rounds start with "how does an LLM actually work?" and move quickly to "how would you make this feature reliable, cheap and measurable?". A 3-year engineer must explain tokens, attention, training stages and sampling in plain words, and then show real judgement on prompting, structured outputs, fine-tuning and — most of all — evaluation. Serving and cost live in file 13; RAG lives in file 25. Syllabus: 2E AI Infrastructure at Scale + "AI Features" checklist (../../syllabus.html).
