Low-level design, OOP and design patterns

329 questions · L1 94 · L2 174 · L3 61 · 15 sub-topics

LLD shows up as a theory round (OOP, SOLID, "which pattern would you use here?") and as a 60–90 minute machine-coding round where you build a working, extensible program and then absorb a new requirement. A 3-year engineer must design clean classes fast, name the pattern only when it earns its place, and handle concurrency on shared state. Topic checklist: ../interview-topics/07-low-level-design.md.
