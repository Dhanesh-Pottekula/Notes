Interview prep · 08

AI engineering, LLMs and agents (P0 for AI roles)

AI-engineer rounds test four things: (1) do you understand how LLMs work well enough to reason about failures, (2) can you build RAG and agents properly, (3) can you evaluate them, (4) can you run them in production (latency, cost, safety). You have real production stories for all four — use them.
