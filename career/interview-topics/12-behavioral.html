12.4 Interview day technique

  • Think aloud; ask clarifying questions; state assumptions
    techinterviewhandbook.org/coding-interview-techniques/ · hellointerview.com "System Design in a Hurry" → "Delivery" (hellointerview.com)
  • When stuck: say what you know, try a smaller case, ask for a hint
    techinterviewhandbook.org/coding-interview-techniques/ · Do: 2 mocks a week on pramp.com or interviewing.io
  • Manage time: check in with the interviewer every 10 minutes in design rounds
    hellointerview.com "System Design in a Hurry" → "Delivery" (hellointerview.com) · Do: a timed 45-minute design mock with a check-in every 10 minutes
  • End each round with a 20-second summary of your solution and trade-offs
    techinterviewhandbook.org/coding-interview-techniques/ · Do: say a 20-second wrap-up (approach, complexity, trade-offs) after every practice problem
  • Remote setup: camera, mic, Excalidraw / CoderPad ready, IDE with your language set up
    excalidraw.com · coderpad.io · Do: test camera, mic and a blank CoderPad the day before
