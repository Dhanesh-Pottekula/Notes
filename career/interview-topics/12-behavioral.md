# 12. Behavioral, HR and the interview process (P0)

Write each story in **STAR** (Situation, Task, Action, Result) — 2 minutes spoken, with a number in the result.
One story can answer several questions; prepare **8–10 strong stories** and map questions to them.

## 12.4 Interview day technique
- [ ] Think aloud; ask clarifying questions; state assumptions ↳ https://www.techinterviewhandbook.org/coding-interview-techniques/ · hellointerview.com "System Design in a Hurry" → "Delivery" (https://www.hellointerview.com)
- [ ] When stuck: say what you know, try a smaller case, ask for a hint ↳ https://www.techinterviewhandbook.org/coding-interview-techniques/ · Do: 2 mocks a week on https://www.pramp.com or https://interviewing.io
- [ ] Manage time: check in with the interviewer every 10 minutes in design rounds ↳ hellointerview.com "System Design in a Hurry" → "Delivery" (https://www.hellointerview.com) · Do: a timed 45-minute design mock with a check-in every 10 minutes
- [ ] End each round with a 20-second summary of your solution and trade-offs ↳ https://www.techinterviewhandbook.org/coding-interview-techniques/ · Do: say a 20-second wrap-up (approach, complexity, trade-offs) after every practice problem
- [ ] Remote setup: camera, mic, Excalidraw / CoderPad ready, IDE with your language set up ↳ https://excalidraw.com · https://coderpad.io · Do: test camera, mic and a blank CoderPad the day before
