# 12. Behavioral, HR and the interview process (P0)

Write each story in **STAR** (Situation, Task, Action, Result) — 2 minutes spoken, with a number in the result.
One story can answer several questions; prepare **8–10 strong stories** and map questions to them.

## 12.3 Logistics and negotiation
- [ ] Salary: current 14 LPA, expected 14–20 LPA — anchor high, give a range, ask for their band first ↳ `application-brief.md` "Numbers to enter" · https://www.techinterviewhandbook.org/negotiation/ · https://www.levels.fyi
  - `L1` What is your current CTC? ↳ state it plainly; split fixed and variable
  - `L1` What is your expected CTC? ↳ give a range; ask their band first
  - `L2` Why is your expectation so much higher than your current salary? ↳ market rate for the role, scope, other offers; stay calm
  - `L2` Our budget is below your range. What now? ↳ ask total comp: ESOPs, bonus, review date; know your floor
- [ ] Notice period: 15 days ↳ `application-brief.md` "Numbers to enter"
  - `L1` What is your notice period? Can you join earlier? ↳ state it; mention early release if possible
  - `L1` Earliest date you can join?
  - `L3` Would you sign a service bond or accept a long notice period?
- [ ] Reasons for job switches (StreetDine → Jigsaw → AiRA) — one line each ↳ `application-brief.md` "Who" (dates and order) · `application-answers.md` "Why are you leaving AiRA?" · `01-your-projects.md` "1.6 Jigsaw" + "1.7 StreetDine"
  - `L2` Why did you join StreetDine while still in college? ↳ founding team, real users, learn by shipping
  - `L2` Why did you move from StreetDine to Jigsaw?
  - `L2` Why did you move from Jigsaw to AiRA? ↳ founding role, AI-first product, more ownership
  - `L2` Your GitHub shows StreetDine commits during your Jigsaw dates. Explain. ↳ part-time on StreetDine after Aug 2024; one honest line
  - `L2` Your resume shows nothing between April and August 2024. What were you doing? ↳ graduation, job search, side projects from that window
  - `L2` Why three jobs in under three years? ↳ one line per move; moving towards something, not away
  - `L3` Three companies in under three years. Why should we believe you'll stay? ↳ a clear reason per move; what you want long term
- [ ] Negotiation: competing offers, joining bonus, ESOPs (vesting, cliff, strike price), fixed vs variable ↳ https://www.techinterviewhandbook.org/negotiation/ · https://www.holloway.com/g/equity-compensation · https://www.techinterviewhandbook.org/understanding-compensation/
  - `L2` How do you compare a startup offer with ESOPs against a higher fixed salary? ↳ vesting, cliff, strike price, liquidity, dilution
  - `L2` Do you have other offers? ↳ honest; share timelines, not necessarily numbers
  - `L2` If your company makes a counter-offer, will you take it? ↳ you decided on reasons, not money; say so
  - `L3` Are you interviewing elsewhere? Where are you in those processes? ↳ honest; timelines, no names needed
  - `L3` You've accepted another offer and we come back with a better one. What do you do?
- [ ] Reference checks; background verification documents ready ↳ `application-brief.md` "Who" (employers, dates, education must match) · Do: keep offer/relieving letters, payslips and degree certificate in one folder; line up one referee each from AiRA and Jigsaw
  - `L2` Can you share payslips and relieving letters for background verification? ↳ keep offer letters, relieving letters, payslips, degree ready
- [ ] Work location: relocation, office days, remote vs hybrid
  - `L1` Are you open to relocating? Which cities?
  - `L1` Are you open to working from the office five days a week?
  - `L1` Remote, hybrid or office?
  - `L2` How do you work remotely vs in an office?

## 12.4 Interview day technique
- [ ] Think aloud; ask clarifying questions; state assumptions ↳ https://www.techinterviewhandbook.org/coding-interview-techniques/ · hellointerview.com "System Design in a Hurry" → "Delivery" (https://www.hellointerview.com)
  - `L1` What is the STAR format, and why do interviewers want it?
- [ ] When stuck: say what you know, try a smaller case, ask for a hint ↳ https://www.techinterviewhandbook.org/coding-interview-techniques/ · Do: 2 mocks a week on https://www.pramp.com or https://interviewing.io
  - `L2` What do you do when you've been stuck for hours?
- [ ] Manage time: check in with the interviewer every 10 minutes in design rounds ↳ hellointerview.com "System Design in a Hurry" → "Delivery" (https://www.hellointerview.com) · Do: a timed 45-minute design mock with a check-in every 10 minutes
  - `L2` How much time do you spend on each phase of a 45-minute design?
- [ ] End each round with a 20-second summary of your solution and trade-offs ↳ https://www.techinterviewhandbook.org/coding-interview-techniques/ · Do: say a 20-second wrap-up (approach, complexity, trade-offs) after every practice problem
- [ ] Remote setup: camera, mic, Excalidraw / CoderPad ready, IDE with your language set up ↳ https://excalidraw.com · https://coderpad.io · Do: test camera, mic and a blank CoderPad the day before
