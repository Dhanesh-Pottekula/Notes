Hands-On Large Language Models

The most visual explanation of what is inside an LLM, then how to use pretrained models (classification, clustering, prompting, agents, RAG, multimodal) and how to train your own embedding and generation models.

One study page per chapter, in the book's order. Each page starts with a map of how the chapter fits together, then walks every section in plain words, with every figure from the book, an analogy, an interactive visual, an "Interview angle" box, and a "check yourself" quiz at the end.
