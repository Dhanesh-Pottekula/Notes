The LLM Bookshelf

Study pages for the three books that matter most for backend and AI engineer interviews (LLM apps, RAG, agents). Read them in this order.

Reading order

1. How LLMs work→2. Building AI apps→3. One full production system
  1. 1Hands-On Large Language ModelsAlammar & Grootendorst. Tokens, embeddings, attention and the Transformer, then prompting, agents, RAG, and fine-tuning. Start with chapters 1–3, 6, 7, 8. 14 pages
  2. 2AI EngineeringChip Huyen. The main reference for AI engineer roles: evaluation, prompting, RAG and agents, finetuning, inference optimization, architecture. The basis of AI system-design rounds. 12 pages
  3. 3LLM Engineer's HandbookIusztin & Labonne. One end-to-end production LLM system: data, RAG pipelines, SFT and DPO, evaluation, inference, deployment, LLMOps. Good material for "design a RAG system". 13 pages

Each page has an "Interview angle" box per section with an answer you can say out loud, and a "Check yourself" quiz. See also interview-prep-subjects.md.
