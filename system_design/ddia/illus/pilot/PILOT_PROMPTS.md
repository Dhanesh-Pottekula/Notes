# Pilot prompts — DDIA ch05 Replication

Two candidate styles, same four analogies (taken from the page's "Picture it" boxes).
All images: 3:2 landscape, plain off-white background (so they sit in a rounded frame in both
light and dark mode), only the listed labels.

Prompts are deliberately long and detailed (user preference): every prompt states the scene, each
character and prop, composition left-to-right, lighting and mood, exact label text and placement,
then the style block. `ch05_leader_follower_A.webp` was made from an earlier short prompt and is
kept only for comparison; the `_A2` / `_B` files use the detailed prompts below.

Each prompt sent to Gemini = "Generate an image. " + scene + " " + style block.

## Style A — flat vector editorial
> Style: flat vector editorial illustration in the manner of a high-quality science magazine
> explainer. Clean rounded geometric shapes, no outlines, subtle paper-grain texture, soft
> drop-shadows only under objects. Limited palette: deep teal (#1f6f78), warm amber (#e0a33a),
> muted plum (#7a4e7e), soft coral accents, warm greys, on a plain warm off-white background
> (#f6f1e7) that fills the whole frame edge to edge. Friendly, simply drawn Indian characters with
> minimal facial detail but expressive posture. Rich environmental detail in the props, but a
> calm, uncluttered composition with clear visual flow and generous margins. Labels in a clean
> bold geometric sans-serif, dark charcoal, spelled exactly as given, each appearing once. No
> other words, numbers only where specified, no watermark, no logos, no brand names. 3:2 landscape.

## Style B — pencil and watercolour sketch
> Style: detailed hand-drawn pencil and fine ink illustration with loose, luminous watercolour
> washes, like a page from a beautifully kept science notebook or a travel sketchbook. Visible
> confident pencil construction lines, cross-hatching in the shadows, watercolour pooling at the
> edges of shapes. Limited palette of teal, amber and plum washes with warm grey shading on plain
> off-white cotton paper that fills the whole frame. Charming, slightly playful Indian characters
> with expressive posture. Rich, specific props and environmental detail, but a clear readable
> composition with generous margins. Labels hand-lettered in neat dark-ink capitals, spelled
> exactly as given, each appearing once. No other words, numbers only where specified, no
> watermark, no logos, no brand names. 3:2 landscape.

## The four scenes

### 1. leader_follower (section: Leaders and Followers)
A wide, slightly elevated three-quarter view of a sunny Indian cricket stadium during a match.
In the lower-left foreground, the official scorer — a middle-aged man in a cream half-sleeve
shirt and spectacles — sits at a small wooden desk under a striped canopy, pen in hand, carefully
writing into a large open ledger-style scorebook; a stack of pencils, an eraser and a steel
tumbler of tea sit beside it. This is the single source of truth. From the scorebook, three
clean curved arrows sweep up and to the right to three copies of the same score "214/3":
(1) a tall stadium scoreboard with lit digits above the stands, (2) a flat-screen TV in a living
room inset bubble where a family watches from a sofa, and (3) a radio commentator in a small
glass booth wearing headphones and speaking into a microphone, with sound waves going out to a
transistor radio held by a man on a scooter. Crowds in the stands in the background, a sliver of
green pitch with tiny players. Bright midday light, cheerful mood. Labels: "LEADER" directly
under the scorer's desk; "FOLLOWERS" once, in a gentle arc above the three copies.

### 2. sync_async (section: Synchronous Versus Asynchronous Replication)
A split-panel illustration divided down the middle by a thin vertical line, same young office
worker (late 20s, blue checked shirt, laptop bag) in both halves. LEFT panel: inside a busy
Indian post office with a wooden counter, steel grille and a wall clock reading ten past four,
he stands at the registered-post window, letter handed over, waiting with arms folded and a
slightly impatient face while the clerk stamps a pink acknowledgement card; a short queue forms
behind him and a dotted line shows the letter travelling to a distant house and the card coming
back. RIGHT panel: outside on a leafy street, the same man drops a letter into a classic red
cylindrical Indian post box and walks away briskly and relaxed, already talking on his phone and
holding a coffee cup; a faint question-mark cloud floats above the post box to show nobody yet
knows if the letter arrived. Soft afternoon light, warm and calm. Labels: "SYNC" at the top of
the left panel, "ASYNC" at the top of the right panel.

### 3. new_follower (section: Setting Up New Followers)
A warm, sunlit coaching-class classroom seen from the side. In the foreground left, a student
who joined late — a teenage girl with a backpack still on — stands at a photocopy machine
copying a thick borrowed notebook; a neat stack of warm, freshly copied pages sits on the tray
with a yellow sticky note on top reading "page 47". Behind her, in the middle ground and right,
the class is in full flow: a teacher in a kurta writes equations on a large green blackboard,
and a row of students at wooden benches copy notes live, pens moving. A bold curved arrow runs
from the photocopied stack toward the live class, showing her catching up from page 47 to the
current lesson. Ceiling fan, wall calendar, tiffin boxes on the benches for detail. Bright
morning light from tall windows. Labels: "SNAPSHOT" on or just above the photocopied stack;
"CATCH UP" along the curved arrow.

### 4. read_your_writes (section: Reading Your Own Writes)
A small, colourful Indian kirana shop counter seen from the customer's side. On the left, a young
man in a hoodie holds his phone close, eyebrows raised and worried, because his phone screen
shows an empty transaction list with a small spinning loader. Across the counter on the right,
the friendly shopkeeper with a moustache smiles and turns his own phone toward the customer,
showing a big green tick. Behind the counter: shelves of colourful packets, glass jars of
sweets, a hanging bunch of bananas and a QR-code stand on the counter (plain pattern, no brand).
In the upper corner, a small inset bubble shows two server cabinets: the left one up to date and
glowing, the right one lagging behind with a small hourglass icon, a dotted arrow between them.
Warm evening shop lighting. Label: "PAID" once, near the green tick on the shopkeeper's phone.
