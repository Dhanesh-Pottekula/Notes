Designing Data-Intensive Applications

One study page per chapter, in the book's order. Each page starts with a map of how the chapter's argument fits together, then walks every section in plain words, with every figure from the book, an analogy, a worked example you can click through, and a "check yourself" quiz at the end.

How the whole book fits together

Part I: one machine→Part II: many machines→Part III: data derived from other data

Part I covers the ideas that apply even when your data sits on one machine: what "good" means for a data system, how you model data, how a database stores and finds it on disk, and how data is encoded as it changes over time. Part II spreads data across machines (copies, splits, transactions, everything that goes wrong on a network, and how machines agree anyway). Part III builds systems out of other systems: batch jobs and streams that turn one dataset into another.

This PDF is the early-release edition: it has chapters 1–11. The final book's Chapter 12, "The Future of Data Systems", is not in it, so there is no page for it here.
