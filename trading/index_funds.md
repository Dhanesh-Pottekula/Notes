# Index Funds — the "Do Nothing" Benchmark

Why it matters: any trading strategy has to beat an index fund after costs and tax, or the index fund wins
(see `trader_returns_reality.md`). Education, not investment advice.

## 1. What an index fund is

- An **index** is a list of stocks with set weights, e.g. Nifty 50 = the 50 biggest NSE companies weighted by free-float market value.
- An **index fund** simply **buys every stock in the index in the same weights** and holds them. No fund manager picking stocks.
- When the index changes (a company drops out, another joins), the fund copies the change.
- You get the **market's return minus a small fee**.

## 2. Two ways to hold one

| | Index mutual fund | ETF (exchange-traded fund) |
|---|---|---|
| How you buy | From the fund house / an MF app, at the day's NAV | On the stock exchange like a share, through a demat account |
| SIP (monthly auto-invest) | Easy | Possible via some brokers |
| Yearly fee (expense ratio) | Low (direct plans usually a fraction of 1%) | Usually even lower |
| Watch out for | Choose the **direct** plan, not regular | **Liquidity**: check the bid-ask spread and volume, buy liquid ETFs |

**Tracking error / difference:** how closely the fund follows the index. Lower is better — compare it along with the fee.

## 3. Common Indian indices

| Index | What's inside | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| **Nifty 50** / **Sensex** (30) | Largest companies | Lowest of these (still falls 30–50% in crashes) |
| **Nifty Next 50** | Companies ranked 51–100 | Higher |
| **Nifty 100 / 200 / 500** | Broader: large + mid (+ small for 500) | Medium |
| **Nifty Midcap 150 / Smallcap 250** | Mid / small companies | High; bigger swings |
| **Factor / "smart beta"** indices, e.g. **Nifty 200 Momentum 30** | Rule-based picks: momentum, low volatility, quality, value | Varies; momentum can lag badly for long stretches |

Nifty 200 Momentum 30 is the real-world version of the momentum rotation strategy I backtested (it made 14.6%/yr since Oct 2020
vs 35% in my survivorship-biased backtest). Several fund houses run index funds/ETFs on it — a way to get the strategy without trading it.

## 4. Why index funds are hard to beat

- **76% of Indian large-cap active funds lagged their index over 10 years** (SPIVA India, Dec 2025) — professionals with research teams.
- Very low costs, no stock-picking mistakes, no emotion, no time needed.
- Long run, Nifty 50 has returned roughly 11–13% a year before tax — with big falls on the way.

## 5. Tax (equity funds, India)

- Held < 1 year: short-term gain taxed at 20%.
- Held ≥ 1 year: long-term gain taxed at 12.5% on gains above ₹1.25 lakh a year.
- Compare with trading: every short swing trade is short-term (20%); intraday profit is business income at slab rate.

## 6. How to choose (checklist)

1. Which index (risk level you can sit through)?
2. Direct plan / low expense ratio.
3. Low tracking difference over 1–3 years.
4. For ETFs: enough daily volume and a tight bid-ask spread.
5. Big, established fund house.
