## 1. The strategy

**Daily chart indicators:** Rate of Change **20** · EMA **50** · MACD **12, 26, 9** · Stochastic RSI **3, 3, 14, 14** (all on the close; during the day the live price counts as today's close).

**BUY** (delivery/CNC):
- At **2:15 pm**, if all of these are true → buy at the 2:15 pm price:
  1. ROC(20) above **7%**
  2. price above the **50 EMA**
  3. MACD histogram **bigger than yesterday's** bar
  4. Stoch RSI **blue crossed above orange** (yesterday blue ≤ orange) with **blue below 50**
- If not all true at 2:15 pm → keep watching until the close and buy **the moment** all four become true.

**SELL:** the moment the **blue line touches the orange line** — later the same afternoon or on any later day (at the open if the stock opens below that level). No stop-loss, no target.

**Money rules (₹1 lakh total):**
- Start with **₹1,00,000**. Every profit/loss goes back into the pot, so trade sizes grow or shrink.
- Up to **4 stocks at a time**; each new buy gets **1/4 of the current total money** (cash + money in open trades), never more than the free cash.
- Several signals at once → buy the one with the **strongest ROC(20)** first. One position per stock.
- Shares = amount ÷ price, rounded down (skip if not even 1 share). Money from a sale is usable from the next day (a sale at the open frees it the same day).
- **FYERS charges** on every trade at its real size: held overnight = delivery charges (brokerage ₹20 or 0.3%/order, STT 0.1% buy+sell, NSE 0.0030699%, SEBI ₹10/crore, IPFT, stamp 0.015% buy, GST 18%, DP ₹14.75 on the sale); sold the same day = intraday charges. No slippage, no income tax.

Buy times: the 1-hour candles show *which hour* a buy happened ("2:15–3:15 pm"); for a buy inside an hour, the cautious assumption is used for a dip later in that same hour (counts as a sell).

## 8. Why ₹1 lakh does worse than the per-stock test

The earlier test gave every signal its own ₹1 lakh (408 trades, +₹194,175). With one ₹1 lakh pot:

| | Trades | Avg move per trade | FYERS charges per trade | Net per trade | Win % |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Every signal, ₹1L each | 408 | +0.73% | 0.25% | +0.48% | 35.3% |
| Taken by the ₹1L portfolio | 329 | +0.62% | 0.38% | +0.24% | 31.3% |
| Skipped (all slots/cash in use) | 79 | +1.18% | | | 46.8% |

- **The skipped signals were the best ones.** Signals come in bursts on strong market days; with all slots full, those are the ones you miss.
- **Small trades pay relatively more charges:** about ₹101 on a ₹26,564 trade (0.38%), because brokerage (₹20/order) and the DP charge (₹14.75) are flat amounts.
- So the strategy needs more money (more slots) to work as tested; with ₹1 lakh it beats a savings account only slightly.

## 10. Caveats

- Backtest on past data with current Nifty 50 members (stocks that joined the index because they rose are included).
- The settings and the 2:15 pm rule were chosen after looking at 2023–26 results, so real results can be worse.
- Prices are TradingView's, adjusted for later bonuses/splits; the ₹ results are the same either way.
- Buys inside an hour use the exact price where all conditions first hold; a real order may fill a little higher.
- No income tax (short-term gains are taxed), no slippage, no interest on idle cash.
